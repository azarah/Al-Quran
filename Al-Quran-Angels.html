The Qur'an names a few angels and describes many by office (revelation, death, record, Throne, Hell). It does not print a numbered hierarchy like a roster. The rank column here is a study order based on how the text itself elevates each role — Jibril as the Spirit who brings the revelation first, then others by the gravity of their charge. Angels known only from later tradition (Israfil, Munkar, Nakir) are omitted.
