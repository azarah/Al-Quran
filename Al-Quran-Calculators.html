These calculators follow numbers and fractions stated in the Qur'an (inheritance shares, waiting periods, makeup days, one-fifth of booty, oath expiation). Where the Book leaves an amount open — a “reasonable” gift, a meal for the needy — you supply a local figure. They are for study and clarity, not a substitute for a qualified scholar in disputed cases.

Inheritance shares

Fixed portions in 4:11, 4:12, and 4:176, after debts and bequests. Among children (and residuary siblings), a male receives twice a female share.
