Waṣiyya to parents and next of kin according to reasonable usage — 2:180 · 5:106

“It is prescribed, when death approaches any of you, if he leave any goods that he make a bequest to parents and next of kin, according to reasonable usage…” 2:180

Bequests come before fixed inheritance shares (4:11–12). Changing a heard bequest wrongfully is sin (2:181). Take two just witnesses (5:106).

Housing / Dwelling Agreement

Homes as rest; permission to enter; lodging according to means — 16:80 · 24:27–29 · 65:6 · 4:29

“It is Allah Who made your habitations homes of rest and quiet for you…” 16:80

The Qur’an does not name a modern “lease,” but it frames dwellings as sakana (rest), forbids entering others’ houses without permission, allows fair lodging, and requires mutual good-will in property dealings (4:29). This form records a dwelling arrangement on those principles.

1. Parties

2. The dwelling

3. Covenants of the dwelling

  • وَاللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُمْ مِنْ بُيُوتِكُمْ سَكَنًا
    The house is for rest and quiet; neither party shall turn it into harm or fear without right.
    16:80
  • Enter not houses other than your own until you have asked permission and saluted those in them; if asked to go back, go back.
    24:27–28
  • No fault to enter non-living spaces that serve a use for you, by agreement.
    24:29
  • If this lodging is for ʿiddah: live in the same style as the provider lives, according to means; annoy not so as to restrict.
    65:6
  • Any payment or exchange of benefit is by mutual good-will — not devouring wealth wrongfully.
    4:29
  • If written as a deferred obligation (rent over a term), reduce it to writing and take witnesses.
    2:282
