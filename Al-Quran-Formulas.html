Mercy, Forgiveness & Laws

Scales, paths, and prescribed limits from Al-Quran.txt
study app · calculators · contracts · names of Allah · full text

These are study formulas taken from explicit wording in the Book — not a ledger of your soul. Numbers (tenfold, seven hundred) are lower bounds or parables of increase; Allah multiplies further for whom He wills. Use them to remember proportion: good is amplified, evil is not amplified beyond itself, and the door of mercy stays open for the repentant.

Laws are the Book’s explicit prescriptions and fixed shares — qisāṣ, inheritance, ḥudūd numbers, booty fifth, written debt, and “limits ordained by Allah.” Full inheritance and ʿiddah math live in Calculators; written forms in Contracts.

Reward Sin 6:160

good → ≥ 10×  ·  evil → 1× (like itself)

مَنْ جَاءَ بِالْحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُ عَشْرُ أَمْثَالِهَا ۖ وَمَنْ جَاءَ بِالسَّيِّئَةِ فَلَا يُجْزَىٰ إِلَّا مِثْلَهَا

He that doeth good shall have ten times as much to his credit: He that doeth evil shall only be recompensed according to his evil: no wrong shall be done unto (any of) them.

Reward 2:261

1 grain → 7 ears × 100 = 700  ·  then Allah multiplies further

مَثَلُ الَّذِينَ يُنْفِقُونَ أَمْوَالَهُمْ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنْبَتَتْ سَبْعَ سَنَابِلَ فِي كُلِّ سُنْبُلَةٍ مِائَةُ حَبَّةٍ

The parable of those who spend their substance in the way of Allah is that of a grain of corn: it groweth seven ears, and each ear Hath a hundred grains. Allah giveth manifold increase to whom He pleaseth…

Reward Mercy 2:245

beautiful loan → doubled, then multiplied many times

Who is he that will loan to Allah a beautiful loan, which Allah will double unto his credit and multiply many times? It is Allah that giveth (you) Want or plenty…

Reward Mercy 4:40

even an atom’s weight of good is not wronged · Allah doubles it and gives from His presence a great reward

Allah is never unjust in the least degree: If there is any good (done), He doubleth it, and giveth from His own presence a great reward.

Repentance Mercy 25:70

repent + believe + righteous work → evil deeds exchanged for good

إِلَّا مَنْ تَابَ وَآمَنَ وَعَمِلَ عَمَلًا صَالِحًا فَأُولَٰئِكَ يُبَدِّلُ اللَّهُ سَيِّئَاتِهِمْ حَسَنَاتٍ

Unless he repents, believes, and works righteous deeds, for Allah will change the evil of such persons into good, and Allah is Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

Mercy Repentance 39:53

transgression against self ≠ despair  ·  Allah forgives all sins (for the turning servant)

قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَىٰ أَنْفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِنْ رَحْمَةِ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا

Say: “O my Servants who have transgressed against their souls! Despair not of the Mercy of Allah: for Allah forgives all sins: for He is Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful.”

Turn toward your Lord before the punishment comes (39:54). Despair is refused; return is commanded.
Repentance 3:135–136 · 4:17–18 · 4:110

shameful deed / self-wrong → remember Allah → ask forgiveness → do not persist knowingly

And those who, having done something to be ashamed of, or wronged their own souls, earnestly bring Allah to mind, and ask for forgiveness for their sins… and are never obstinate in persisting knowingly in (the wrong) they have done. (3:135)

  • Remembered Allah after the fault
  • Asked forgiveness (who forgives sins except Allah?)
  • Not persisting knowingly
  • Repenting soon after ignorance — not delaying until death’s face (4:17–18)
  • If evil or self-wrong done: sought Allah’s forgiveness (4:110)
Sin & limit Mercy 4:48 · 4:116

shirk (associating partners) → not forgiven if one dies thereon  ·  lesser than that → forgiven for whom He wills

Allah forgiveth not that partners should be set up with Him; but He forgiveth anything else, to whom He pleaseth… (4:48)

This is the Book’s hard boundary: tawḥīd first. Other sins remain under hope and His will — never a license to sin, always a call to return.
Sin & limit 42:30

affliction that befalls you ↔ what your hands have earned  ·  yet He forgives much

Whatever misfortune happens to you, is because from the things your hands have wrought, and for many (of them) He grants forgiveness.

Formula of accountability with a mercy clause: consequence is real; forgiveness is larger than many of the faults.
Mercy 2:286

burden ≤ capacity  ·  soul keeps what it earns of good and ill

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا ۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتْ وَعَلَيْهَا مَا اكْتَسَبَتْ

On no soul doth Allah Place a burden greater than it can bear. It gets every good that it earns, and it suffers every ill that it earns… “Blot out our sins, and grant us forgiveness. Have mercy on us…”

Mercy as design: duty scaled to strength; then the prayer for wiping sins, forgiveness, and mercy.
Mercy 7:156

My mercy extendeth to all things  ·  written especially for those who fear, give, and believe

…and My Mercy extendeth to all things. That (mercy) I shall ordain for those who do right, and practise regular charity, and those who believe in Our signs.

Universal reach, then special writing for the God-fearing — mercy is both ocean and appointment.
Mercy 15:49 · 15:56

tell My servants: I am Oft-forgiving, Most Merciful  ·  despair of His mercy = lost

Tell My servants that I am indeed the Oft-forgiving, Most Merciful… And who despaireth of the mercy of his Lord, but such as go astray? (15:49, 15:56)

Identity of the Lord toward servants: forgiveness and mercy named first. Despair is itself a straying.
Repentance Law 5:89

broken deliberate oath → expiation: feed 10 · or clothe 10 · or free a slave · else 3 fasts

Allah will not call you to account for what is futile in your oaths, but He will call you to account for your deliberate oaths…

Numeric expiation lives in the Calculators → Oath expiation tool. Formula: intention matters; careless speech is not billed the same as a deliberate oath.
Mercy Repentance 9:104 · 42:25

Allah accepts repentance · takes charity · He answers His servants and remits their evil

Know they not that Allah doth accept repentance from His votaries and receives their gifts of charity, and that Allah is verily He, the Oft-Returning, Most Merciful? (9:104)

And He is the One that accepts repentance from His servants and forgives sins (42:25). Acceptance is His attribute; offering is yours.
Law Mercy 2:178

qisāṣ in murder: free for free · slave for slave · woman for woman · remission → fair settlement + gratitude

كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْقِصَاصُ فِي الْقَتْلَى … فَمَنْ عُفِيَ لَهُ مِنْ أَخِيهِ شَيْءٌ فَاتِّبَاعٌ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ

The law of equality is prescribed to you in cases of murder… If any remission is made by the brother of the slain, then grant any reasonable demand, and compensate him with handsome gratitude — a concession and a Mercy from your Lord.

Equality is the baseline; remission is permitted and counted as mercy. Whoever transgresses after that faces a grave penalty.
Law 5:45

life↔life · eye↔eye · nose · ear · tooth · wounds equal for equal · remit retaliation → charity = expiation

We ordained therein for them: “Life for life, eye for eye… and wounds equal for equal.” But if any one remits the retaliation by way of charity, it is an act of atonement for himself.

The Book pairs strict proportion with a lawful exit: charity instead of taking the exact retaliation.
Law 4:11

among children: male share = 2× female · daughters-only cases → ½ or ⅔ · parents sixths · after debts & bequest

لِلذَّكَرِ مِثْلُ حَظِّ الْأُنْثَيَيْنِ

To the male, a portion equal to that of two females… (distribution) after the payment of legacies and debts. These are settled portions ordained by Allah.

Full farāʾiḍ with spouses and parents: Calculators → Inheritance.
Law Reward 8:41

booty acquired → ⅕ (khums) to Allah, Messenger, kin, orphans, needy, wayfarer

Out of all the booty that ye may acquire, a fifth share is assigned to Allah, and to the Messenger, and to near relatives, orphans, the needy, and the wayfarer.

Law Sin & limit 5:38

thief (male or female) → cut off hands · exemplary punishment from Allah

As to the thief, Male or female, cut off his or her hands: a punishment by way of example, from Allah, for their crime.

5:39 opens repentance after wrongdoing — study the pair for warning and door together.
Law 24:2

adultery/fornication (both) → 100 lashes each · believers witness · no pity overriding Allah’s limit

The woman and the man guilty of adultery or fornication — flog each of them with a hundred stripes… let a party of the Believers witness their punishment.

Fixed count in the text; surrounding sūra treats evidence and social order — not a courtroom guide here.
Law 2:275

trade permitted · usury forbidden · cease after admonition → past pardoned · return → Fire

Allah hath permitted trade and forbidden usury. Those who after receiving direction from their Lord, desist, shall be pardoned for the past… those who repeat are companions of the Fire.

Written debt without riba: Contracts → Debt (2:282).
Law 2:229–230

ṭalāq permissible twice → then hold fairly or release kindly · third path needs new nikāḥ (2:230)

A divorce is only permissible twice: after that, the parties should either hold Together on equitable terms, or separate with kindness… These are the limits ordained by Allah.

ʿIddah and housing: Contracts → Divorce & ʿIddah · Marriage form.
Law 2:282

deferred debt → write it · scribe just · witnesses · no harm to scribe or witness

When ye deal with each other, in transactions involving future obligations in a fixed period of time, reduce them to writing… and get two witnesses, out of your own men.

Printable clauses: Contracts → Debt.
Law 5:3

forbidden: carrion · blood · swine flesh · what is invoked besides Allah · strangled · beaten · fallen · gored · devoured by beasts (except what ye slaughter) · what is sacrificed on stones

Forbidden to you (for food) are: dead meat, blood, the flesh of swine, and that on which hath been invoked the name of other than Allah… (full list in 5:3).

Law of permission follows: “This day are (all) things good and pure made lawful unto you” — read the whole verse for exceptions (game, sea, necessity).
