Genealogy. Solid purple lines are parent → child stated in the text (Azar–Ibrahim, Ibrahim–Ismail/Ishaq, Ishaq–Yaqub, Yaqub–Yusuf, Dawud–Sulayman, Zakariya–Yahya, Imran–Maryam, Maryam–Isa). Map. Markers sit at conventional coordinates for places the Book names (Makkah, Yathrib, Badr, Egypt, Midian, Sinai, Babylon, Saba, al-Ahqaf, al-Hijr, Mount Judi, and others). Click a pin or chip for the verse cue.

Destroyed or punished. A separate view gathers peoples and towns the Qur'an says were seized in this world — Flood, wind, blast, overturned cities, drowning of Pharaoh, the Arim flood, and more — with the form of punishment and the messenger sent to them where named.

These are peoples and sites the Qur'an recounts as seized in the world after they rejected their messengers — not the Fire of the Hereafter. Locations are approximate; some names (ar-Rass, al-Ayka) have uncertain geography and are plotted near related lands.
