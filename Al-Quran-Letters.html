Every Arabic root in the Book begins with a letter. Grouping those roots by first letter reveals a theme — a field of meaning that letter tends to open: God and humanity for ʾalif, mercy and lordship for rāʾ, speech for qāf, and so on.

Below, each letter shows its theme, a signature Quranic keyword, and the sense clusters of roots that begin with it. Counts come from Quranic Arabic Corpus morphology. Order is by total root occurrences.
