4. Solemn covenant from the Book

The Qur’an calls marriage a solemn covenant taken from the husband (مِيثَاقًا غَلِيظًا) — 4:21. The parties affirm:

  • وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ
    To live together on a footing of kindness and equity.
    4:19
  • وَلَهُنَّ مِثْلُ الَّذِي عَلَيْهِنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ
    Women shall have rights similar to the rights against them, according to what is equitable.
    2:228
  • هُنَّ لِبَاسٌ لَكُمْ وَأَنْتُمْ لِبَاسٌ لَهُنَّ
    They are your garments and ye are their garments — mutual cover, warmth, and honour.
    2:187
  • وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُمْ مَوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً
    To seek the love and mercy Allah places between spouses; to dwell in tranquillity.
    30:21
  • Not to take back the dower by harshness or slander; the covenant stands after intimacy.
    4:20–21
  • If separation becomes necessary, to hold together on equitable terms or separate with kindness — without injury.
    2:229 · 2:231
