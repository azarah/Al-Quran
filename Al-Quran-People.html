Believers & Non-believers

Types of people the Qur'an names, ordered by role
study app · roots · letter context · names of Allah · angels · genealogy & places · full text

The Book sorts people by faith and conduct, not by tribe alone. Believers run from the foremost drawn near, through the God-fearing and the submitters. Non-believers run from open rejection and idolatry to hypocrisy and wrongdoing. Rank here is a study order within each side — how the text itself elevates or condemns the type — not a census of souls.

Surah al-Waqiʿah (56) names three companies on that Day: the foremost, the companions of the right, and the companions of the left. Those threads appear below with the other standing labels of the Book.
