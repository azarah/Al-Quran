Game design pitch · from the Al-Quran study suite

As-Sirat — The Straight Path

A respectful knowledge RPG: you are a seeker, not a prophet. You travel Qurʾanic places, learn from messengers as mentors, face moral forks, and unlock roots of Arabic as living abilities.

Core fantasy

You wake as a traveler whose Book of Signs is blank. Across ages and lands named in the Qurʾan, you collect clear proofs (āyāt), choose between paths of belief and denial, and grow in knowledge — until the Straight Path is no longer a map, but who you have become.

SeekerPlayer role
MentorsProphets (never playable)

Tone rules

  • Prophets, Maryam, and angels are guides and witnesses — never bosses, loot drops, or romance options.
  • “Combat” is trial, patience, argument, and refuge — not gore or mockery of sacred figures.
  • Every quest cites real verses from your corpus; wrong answers cost Clarity, not blasphemy jokes.

Genre mix

What it feels like

  • Exploration RPG + moral dialogue tree
  • Language-learning as magic system
  • Chapter structure like Surahs / story cycles
  • Party of virtues, not murder-hobos

What it is not

  • Not a prophet simulator
  • Not a jihad action shooter
  • Not trivia with XP slapped on
  • Not sectarian propaganda campaigns

One-sentence sell

Walk the lands of the Reminder, learn the roots of speech, and choose — again and again — who you stand with when signs arrive.

Ruined zones

Optional hard areas: People of Nuh, ʿAd, Thamud, Cities of Lut, Pharaoh’s host, Companions of the Elephant. You do not “defeat” them — you witness, recover buried āyāt, and refuse the same mistake.

Genealogy as living lore

Your prophetic tree becomes NPC relationship memory: meeting Yusuf soft-locks Egypt chapters; knowing Ibrahim’s line opens Makkah side quests. Edges (parent / sibling / guardian) power “who belongs with whom?” puzzles in dialogue.

Three meters

Core

Clarity

How clearly you see signs. Raised by correct verse recall, root mastery, and keeping promises. Low Clarity blurs map labels and weakens counsel from mentors.

Moral

Stance

A spectrum toward believer or denier archetypes from your People page (26 types). Choices tag you: grateful, patient, arrogant, mocking, just, heedless — not a Good/Evil bar with cartoon horns.

Language

Roots

Skill tree of Arabic roots and letter themes. Learning ر ح م unlocks Mercy dialogue options; ص ب ر unlocks Patience trials. Letter keywords + TTS become tutorial “utterances.”

Encounters (not slash combat)

Trial of Speech

Debate denier archetypes using roots you unlocked. Wrong root = Clarity damage. Angels of office may intervene as one-time counsel — never as player weapons.

Trial of Deed

Scenario puzzles from your Calculators: inheritance shares, oath expiation, iddah waiting — applied as village quests with verse-cited solutions.

Trial of Patience

Hold stance under pressure (Ayyub-pattern nights, Yusuf prison chapters). Mini-games: steady breath meter, refuse three escalating taunts.

Hunt of Signs

Verse-hunt using Study search: given English/Arabic fragment, find the ayah in the world (inscribed stones, dreams, mentor speech).

Book of Signs

Collected āyāt, names of Allah (99), prophet seals, and letter themes fill a living codex. Endgame: the Book is complete enough to walk a final Sirat sequence where Stance + Roots decide which companions walk with you.

Sample quest: The Well at Midian

Act beat

You arrive thirsty. Two flocks wait; women delay. Choices tag Stance (help / ignore / demand pay). Helping unlocks Shuayb’s counsel and roots of watering and patience.

Side puzzle: a merchant short-weights grain. Trial of Deed uses fairness judgment. Success adds Companion Adl; failure opens a ruined Midian echo later.

Verses cited on success — same pattern as your study tools. Playable now in Study → As-Sirat.

End states

Guided

High Clarity + believer Stance + full Mentors witnessed. Sirat is bright; Book of Signs becomes a permanent in-app study deck.

Scattered

High denier tags or abandoned roots. Map fragments; mentors speak in warning only. Replay encouraged — not an “evil ending” meme.

Build path

Phase A

Vertical slice (done): Midian hub — map, mentor, Stance fork, roots, verse hunts, Book of Signs.

Phase B

Spine: Egypt → Sinai → Makkah. Genealogy gates. People-type debates.

Phase C

Depth: Ruined zones, Asma seals, calculator quests, full Sirat ending.

Why this fits

As-Sirat is not a separate product bolted on — it is your Study, Roots, Genealogy, People, Angels, and Calculators turned into one continuous journey. Players finish with more knowledge of the Qurʾan, not less.
